# fr
⏸ met une boucle en pause sans la terminer. ▶ la reprend au tempo et à la série atteints, ⏹ la termine. En mode strict, une fausse note colore aussi en rouge la note visée.

# en
⏸ pauses a loop without ending it. ▶ takes it up at the tempo and streak it had reached, ⏹ ends it. In strict mode, a wrong note also turns the note it was meant for red.
